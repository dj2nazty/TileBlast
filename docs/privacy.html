TileBlast Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TileBlast mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Information we collect ourselves

TileBlast itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. All gameplay data — your high score, current game state, and daily-reward timestamp — is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences and is never sent to us or any server we operate.

2. Information collected by third-party advertising

TileBlast displays ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To show relevant ads, AdMob may collect and process the following:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier provided by Android)
  • Coarse device information (model, operating system version, language)
  • General location inferred from IP address (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, clicked, or dismissed)

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time: Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device.

3. Children's privacy

TileBlast is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App requests no permissions and stores no personally identifiable information on-device.

4. Permissions

TileBlast requests the INTERNET permission solely to load advertisements through AdMob. No other Android runtime permissions are requested.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because TileBlast stores nothing on a server, there is no data for us to delete. To remove all locally stored data (high score, daily-reward timestamp), uninstall the App or clear its data via Settings → Apps → TileBlast → Storage → Clear data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Effective date" at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
