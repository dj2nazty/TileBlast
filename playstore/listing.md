## Full description (max 4000 chars)
TileBlast is a fast, satisfying block-puzzle game in the spirit of the classics — but with premium glossy graphics, dramatic line-clear bursts, and a streak system that rewards bold plays.

★ HOW IT WORKS
Drag one of three colorful pieces onto the 8×8 grid. Fill a complete row or column to clear it and rack up points. Run out of room and the game ends — your best score gets saved.

★ COMBO MULTIPLIERS
Clear two lines at once for a ×2 STRIKE. Three lines for a ×3 TRIPLE. Four for a ×4 QUAD. Plan your placements and watch the bonus points stack.

★ FEATURES
• Smooth, glossy 3D-beveled tiles
• Eye-popping line-clear explosions with sparkle bursts
• "NEW BEST SCORE!" celebration animation when you set a record
• Daily reward — claim 500 bonus points every day
• Three lives per session
• Saves your all-time best
• No account needed — just open and play

★ FREE TO PLAY
TileBlast is free, supported by short ads between games. No subscriptions, no pay-to-win.

★ PERFECT FOR
A quick break at work. A wind-down before bed. A focus break between meetings. A long flight. Anytime you want a satisfying mental challenge that's easy to start and hard to put down.

Drop. Clear. Combo. Beat your best.
